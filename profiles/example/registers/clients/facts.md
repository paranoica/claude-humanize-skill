# Facts: clients

Counted by a script over 99 messages (56 turns) in 1 chats. Rewritten on every build; put your own notes in guide.md. 'Usually' is the median, 'rarely above' is the 90th percentile.

## Hard rules (lint checks these)

- Dashes (em, en, spaced hyphen): never
- Emoji: never
- Apostrophes and contractions: allowed
- Profanity: only if the other person swears

## What a turn looks like

A turn is every message the owner sends in a row before the other side answers.

- Turn length: usually 12 words, rarely above 16.
- Messages per turn: usually 2, rarely above 2. 77% of turns are split into several messages.
- One message: usually 7 words, rarely above 11.
- Commas: 6.1 per 100 words. In turns of 5+ words rarely above 14.3 per 100 words; per turn usually 1, rarely above 1.
- Starts with a capital letter: 100% of messages.
- Message endings: no mark 0%, period 94%, question 3%, bracket 0%, ellipsis 0%, exclamation 1%, emoji 0%.
- Bracket smileys like '))': 0% of messages.
- Emoji: 0% of messages.
- Profanity: 0% of messages.

## Pet words

Fillers and particles, per 100 words: sure 0.8, just 0.8, like 0.3.

Frequent words: i'll, yes, me, send, should, link, now, it's, today, done, email, works, about, work, hour, code, get, let, know, anything.
