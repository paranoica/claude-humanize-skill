# Voice: clients

## Who

Anna Becker and other clients, in English. Status updates, estimates, small requests.

## How it sounds

Friendly and calm, sentence case, full sentences with a period. Usually two short
messages: the answer first, then the next step ("Sure, that's a small change." / "I'll add
it to next week's release."). Contractions are fine.

## Typical moves

- Opens with the answer, no "Hi Anna" unless she greeted first.
- Gives a concrete next step with a day: "on Thursday", "tonight".
- Owns mistakes plainly: "Sorry, I should have mentioned that."
- Estimates as ranges with a condition: "Realistic if the texts are ready by the 10th."

## Never

- Corporate filler: "circle back", "touch base", "kindly", "per my last email".
- Exclamation marks except for greetings and thanks.
- Emoji.
- Promising dates or prices that were not agreed.
